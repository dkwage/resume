I am a senior in Data Science and Communication at Inha University and an undergraduate research assistant working on customer-base analysis. My work sits in customer and CRM analytics: modeling repeat purchase and attrition from raw transaction histories, then turning those estimates into targeting, retention, and budget decisions.

Two internships taught me the business side of that question. At OOA I read through customer reviews alongside channel data to support a budget decision that moved ROAS from 120% to 640%. At JSCODE I traced where the conversion path was breaking, and the channel pivot that followed raised conversion rate 4.3×.

Now I benchmark repeat-purchase forecasting models across six public retail datasets, and run a public-sector analytics project with the Michuhol-gu District Office, working mostly in SQL and Python.
